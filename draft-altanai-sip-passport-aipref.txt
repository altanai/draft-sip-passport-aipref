 Personal Assertion Token (PASSporT) Extension for AI Usage Preferences
                              in SIP Calls
                draft-altanai-sip-passport-aipref-latest

Abstract

   This document outlines a potential Personal Assertion Token
   (PASSporT) extension for conveying AI usage preferences associated
   with SIP calls, including preferences concerning the use of call data
   for training large language models (LLMs).  It explores binding
   preference information to authenticated call identity.  This initial
   discussion outline leaves the extension design and verification
   procedures to be determined (TBD).

1.  Introduction

   PASSporT [RFC8225] and the STIR authentication procedures for SIP
   [RFC8224] provide a basis for associating signed information with
   calls.  This document explores applying that model to AI usage
   preferences for call data.  The proposed extension is outlined in
   Section 4, with SIP procedures reserved in Section 5.

   TBD: Refine the problem statement, intended status, and initial use
   cases with contributors.

1.1.  Relationship to Existing Work

   AI Preferences for Real-Time Protocol Bindings
   [I-D.altanai-aipref-realtime-protocol-bindings] describes AI
   Preference Tokens (APTs), the SIP AI-Pref header field, SDP bindings,
   and preference updates during a session.  This document explores a
   specific PASSporT binding that could complement those mechanisms and
   the AIPREF vocabulary [I-D.ietf-aipref-vocab].

   The extension structure follows the precedents in [RFC8946] and
   [RFC9795]: claim definitions, SIP authentication and verification
   procedures, and separate IANA registration considerations.

   TBD: Agree on the boundary between the existing real-time bindings
   and this extension, including which mechanisms are reused.

5.  Use in SIP

   The following sections reserve the SIP [RFC3261] procedures for
   authentication and verification services, following [RFC8946] and
   [RFC9795].

5.1.  Authentication Service Behavior

   TBD: Define signer eligibility, PASSporT construction, preference
   attachment, and carriage in the SIP Identity header field.

5.2.  Verification Service Behavior

   TBD: Define signature and credential validation, preference
   retrieval, binding checks, and handling of unsupported, unavailable,
   or invalid preference information.  Distinguish call identity
   verification from preference acceptance and enforcement.

6.  Preference Updates and Call Transitions

   TBD: Specify applicability to mid-dialog updates, agent-to-human
   handoffs, and forwarded or transferred calls, including the
   relationship to [RFC8946].  Define freshness, ordering, and
   supersession without treating an actor change as an automatic change
   to data-use preferences.
